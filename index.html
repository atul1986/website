About

I'm a Professor in the Department of Computer Engineering and Associate Dean – Academics at Ajeenkya D. Y. Patil University, Pune, with more than 17 years of teaching and one year of industry experience.

My research covers the Internet of Things, machine learning, network and cyber security, cloud computing and blockchain. Most of it is applied: detecting crop disease in the field, protecting health records, securing vehicular networks, and making cloud and sensor systems use less energy.

I serve as a reviewer and editor for 49+ Scopus and SCI journals — including IEEE Access, Applied Soft Computing, Biomedical Signal Processing and Control and Taylor & Francis Applied AI — with 280+ peer reviews verified on ORCID. That reviewing has earned me more than 16 years of open access to Scopus, ScienceDirect and Reaxys. I also chair conference sessions and coordinate accreditation work for NBA, NAAC and NIRF.
